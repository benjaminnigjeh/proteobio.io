About ProteoAgent

AI that thinks like a team of proteomics experts

ProteoAgent is a multi-agent AI system purpose-built for proteomics. Rather than a single monolithic model, it deploys a coordinated network of specialized agents — each expert in a distinct step of the proteomics pipeline.

Built on trained spectral search engines, transformer-based PTM characterization, explainable AI for feature discovery, and fine-tuned foundation models for novel property exploration — ProteoAgent brings cutting-edge AI to every layer of the proteomics workflow.

Expert agents collaborate through closed agentic loops, guided by auxiliary agents that enforce quality controls, guard-railing, compliance, and reproducibility at every step.

Capabilities

What ProteoAgent can do

Purpose-built AI technologies working in concert — from raw spectra to biological insight.

⚡

Trained Spectral Search Engines

Purpose-trained search engines deliver ultra-fast spectral matching at scale — orders of magnitude faster than conventional tools, without sacrificing sensitivity or specificity.

High-Speed · Trained Models · MS/MS
🔬

Transformer-Based PTM Characterization

Transformer architectures capture long-range sequence context to precisely localize and classify post-translational modifications — including rare and co-occurring PTMs missed by classical tools.

Transformers · PTM · Site Localization
💡

Explainable AI for Feature Discovery

Our XAI layer surfaces the biological and spectral features driving every prediction — giving researchers transparent, interpretable evidence rather than a black-box score.

XAI · Interpretability · Feature Attribution
🧠

Fine-Tuned Foundation Models

Large foundation models fine-tuned on curated proteomics datasets unlock discovery of novel protein properties, unexpected modifications, and emergent biological signals beyond known databases.

Foundation Models · Novel Properties · Fine-Tuning
🤝

Multi-Agent Orchestration

ProteoAgent coordinates Expert and Auxiliary agents through agentic loops — with built-in guard-railing, error handling, compliance, and quality controls at every step.

Orchestration · Guard-Railing · QC
📝

Automated Reporting

Publication-ready reports with full provenance, statistical summaries, and methods — generated end-to-end with compliance and reproducibility baked in.

Reports · Compliance · Reproducibility
